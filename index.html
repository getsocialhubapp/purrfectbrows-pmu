FAQ

Does it hurt?

Topical numbing is applied before and during the procedure. Most clients describe it as mild scratching or pressure — very manageable.

How long do powder brows last?

Typically 1–3 years depending on skin type, lifestyle, and pigment. Annual touch-ups keep them looking fresh.

What is the healing process like?

Brows look bolder for the first few days, then soften ~30–40% as they heal over 2–4 weeks. Light flaking is normal. Aftercare instructions are provided.

Can you fix my old brow tattoo?

Often, yes — color correction can neutralize grey, blue, or red tones from previous work. Send a clear photo via DM for an honest assessment.

Who isn't a candidate?

Pregnant or nursing clients, and some skin conditions or medications, may require waiting. We'll cover this in your consultation.
